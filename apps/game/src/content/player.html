Player

Move & jump

Player is a platformer character. Rapier's kinematic character controller handles collisions: it detects the ground, steps over small bumps and pushes dynamic bodies out of the way.

  • ← → or A D Move
  • Space, ↑ or W Jump (hold to jump higher)

How it works

  1. Acceleration, not instant speed

    Each step, the horizontal speed moves towards speed at acceleration m/s², and back to 0 at deceleration m/s². In the air, both are multiplied by airControl, so you can steer a jump without stopping dead.

  2. Gravity and jump

    A kinematic body ignores the world's gravity, so the player applies its own (gravity, 40 m/s² by default, for snappy jumps). The jump speed is √(2 · gravity · jumpHeight), which is exactly the speed needed to reach jumpHeight.

  3. Variable height

    If you release the key while still rising, the upward speed is multiplied by jumpCut once. A tap gives a short hop, and holding the key gives the full jump.

  4. Coyote time and jump buffer

    Two small timers make jumping feel fair. Coyote time lets you jump for coyoteTime seconds after walking off a ledge. The jump buffer remembers a press for jumpBuffer seconds, so pressing just before landing still jumps.

Options

All of them are optional. Pass them as new Player(world, { ... }).

OptionDefaultDescription
x, y0, -3.5Start position, in meters. The default stands on Ground.
halfWidth, halfHeight0.5, 1Size of the player's box (half extents).
speed8Top horizontal speed, m/s.
acceleration80m/s² on the ground while a direction key is pressed.
deceleration60m/s² on the ground with no key pressed.
airControl0.6Multiplier for acceleration and deceleration in the air.
gravity40m/s², pulling down.
maxFallSpeed25m/s.
jumpHeight3Meters, for a full jump.
jumpCut0.5Fraction of upward speed kept when the key is released early.
coyoteTime0.1Seconds.
jumpBuffer0.1Seconds.
maxJumps1See Double jump.
dashfalseSee Dash.
wallJumpfalseSee Wall jump.
dropThroughTime0.25Seconds that one-way platforms are ignored after ↓ + jump. See One-way platforms.
maxSlope50Steepest walkable slope, in degrees. See Geometry.
respawnBelow-15Falling below this Y sends the player back to its start position. See State & events for the respawn event.
keyssee belowLists of KeyboardEvent.code values for each action.

Default keys: left: ["ArrowLeft", "KeyA"], right: ["ArrowRight", "KeyD"], jump: ["Space", "ArrowUp", "KeyW"], dash: ["ShiftLeft", "ShiftRight", "KeyX"], down: ["ArrowDown", "KeyS"]. You can override one action without the others, for example keys: { jump: ["KeyZ"] }.
