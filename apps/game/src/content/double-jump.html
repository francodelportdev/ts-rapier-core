Double jump

Set maxJumps: 2 and the player can jump once more in mid-air. The platform here is too high for a single jump.

  • ← → Move
  • Space Jump, then Space again in the air

How it works

  • maxJumps is the number of jumps available before landing. 1 is a normal jump, 2 is a double jump, and 3 or more lets you keep jumping.
  • Every jump in the air resets the upward speed to the full jump speed, so it reaches the same height as a jump from the ground.
  • Walking off a ledge without jumping uses up the ground jump, so you only get the air jumps. Within coyote time, a jump still counts as a ground jump.
  • Landing restores every jump.
