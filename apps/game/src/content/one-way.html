One-way platforms

You jump up through them from below and stand on them from above. Hold ↓ and jump to drop through. Climb the tower, then compare with the solid platform on the right, which blocks you from below.

  • ← → Move
  • Space Jump up through a platform
  • ↓ + Space Drop through

How it works

  1. Mark a platform as one-way

    Add oneWay: true to a Platform, or to any fixed Shape, including tilted ones. One-way platforms are drawn in a lighter gray, and a Platform also gets a bright top edge.

  2. Solid only from above

    For the player, a one-way platform is solid only when the player's bottom edge is above the platform's top surface, at the left side, the center or the right side of the player. Coming from below or the side, the player passes through. One-way platforms are never walls for wall slides.

  3. Drop through

    Pressing jump while holding ↓ (keys.down) on a one-way platform drops through it instead of jumping. For dropThroughTime seconds (0.25 by default), every one-way platform is ignored, so the player doesn't land back on the same one. Dropping uses up the ground jump, just like walking off a ledge. The dropThrough event fires.

Only the player passes through. Other bodies, such as boxes or dynamic shapes, always collide with one-way platforms. player.isOnOneWayPlatform() tells you whether ↓ + jump would drop through.
