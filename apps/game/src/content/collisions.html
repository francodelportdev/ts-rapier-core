Physics

Collisions

The game tells you when two entities start and stop touching, so you can hurt the player, break a crate or play a sound.

  • ← Push the boxes on the left
  • → Walk into the enemy on the right
  • Space Jump

Boxes turn red while something other than the floor touches them. Touching the enemy sends the player back.

Two ways to listen

// Every pair in the game
game.on("collisionStart", ({ a, b }) => {});
game.on("collisionEnd", ({ a, b }) => {});

// Or inside an entity, or a component
class Crate extends Box {
  override onCollisionStart(other: Entity) {}
  override onCollisionEnd(other: Entity) {}
}

Each pair starts once and ends once: when the entities stop touching, or when either one leaves the game. a and b come in no particular order. Hooks and listeners run inside the physics step, where removing entities is safe.

What counts

CaseReported
Boxes landing, stacking or hitting wallsYes
The player standing on the ground or walking into a wallYes
The player pushing a boxYes. A 10 kg player sends a 1 kg box flying, so the contact ends soon after.
A kinematic enemy, such as one with Patrol, walking into the playerYes
Sensors and triggersNo: they have their own enter and exit events (see Sensors & triggers)

game.clear(), which every scene change calls, ends no contacts, just as it emits no entityRemoved. If a hook throws, the error goes to game.on("error") and the game keeps running.
