Player

Checkpoints & knockback

Move the player from your own code: push it when it's hit, change where it comes back, or teleport it.

  • ← → Move
  • Space Jump (twice for a double jump)
  • R Respawn

Walk into the red spikes, cross the pit to the blue flag, then fall into the pit: you come back at the flag. The purple pad sends you to the start.

Methods

MethodWhat it does
knockback(x, y, lock = 0.2)Pushes the player at (x, y) m/s and ignores the arrow keys for lock seconds, so the push isn't cancelled at once. It ends a dash.
setVelocity(x, y)Sets the speed without the lock. Gravity, the ground and the arrow keys act on it from the next step, so a sideways push fades quickly.
setPosition(x, y)A teleport, keeping the speed. It isn't drawn sliding there. Call game.camera.snapToTarget() to move the camera at once too.
setSpawn(x, y) / getSpawn()Where respawn() and falls below respawnBelow send the player.

setVelocity() and knockback() also cancel the jump cut, so releasing jump doesn't shorten an upward push. Every entity has setPosition(), getVelocity() and setVelocity(): velocity moves dynamic bodies, and other bodies ignore it.

In a LevelScene, touching a checkpoint makes it the spawn point, for hazards and falls alike.
